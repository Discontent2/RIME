# RIME v0.012 Mapping Intelligence

v0.012 formalizes the path from visual evidence to musical behavior.

## Musical jurisdiction

### Macro: 8×8 / large structure
Controls or strongly influences:
- tonic stability
- register depth
- bass weight
- large-form tendency
- large persistent/drone structures

### Meso: 16×16 / core + 32×32 structural detail
Controls or strongly influences:
- visual kinetic potential
- groove and phrase energy
- syncopation / transient drive
- directional motion

### Micro: fragile 32×32 detail and transformed evidence
Controls or strongly influences:
- SN76489 hat/noise surface
- small percussion
- ornament
- local FM/noise roughness

Micro detail is intentionally prevented from directly selecting tonic or form.

## Crossmodal priors

- brightness → spectral brightness / register height
- large dark mass → bass weight / low-register occupation
- fine visual detail → PSG/high-frequency musical detail
- visual roughness → local FM/noise roughness
- blur-persistent structure → sustain / drone / foundation
- contrast → accent/transient definition
- directional energy → kinetic potential / propulsion
- hue → timbral/harmonic color, not direct tonic selection

## Equivariance

`--mapping-test` creates controlled transformations while preserving the base identity family. The laboratory locks tonic, mode, RIME Sigil, Genesis groove architecture and form architecture. The surface genome remains free to respond to the visual change.

This is a development instrument, not proof that every mapping is perceptually optimal. Human listening remains essential.
